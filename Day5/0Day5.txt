
Day5: Memory hierarchy 

	register/L1			^
	L2 cache			|fastest
	L3 cache			|smallest
	Main memory(RAM)		|most expensive
	Second memory(SSD & HDD)	|

RAM:
-It needs electric flow continuously
-It calls 'volatile'
-All processes are terminated when the computer is shut down.
-It is located on the motherboard.

!!!RAM speed, CPU cache size, and disk type are just as important as capacity.

Cache hit:
-It finds the requested data in the cache and serves it without accessing the disk.

Cache miss:
-It doesn't find the requested data in the cache and serves on the disc
